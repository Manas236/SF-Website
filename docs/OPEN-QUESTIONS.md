   what the company is known for; the kinds of buildings usually worked on
   (no client names). Two placeholders.

3. **Founder note** (About): a short line about Yogesh Bankar. One placeholder.

   the rating card, or give exact coordinates for a single pin.
7. **Email for enquiries:** which mailbox sends them and its SMTP settings
   (for Gmail, an App Password). Until then enquiries are only in
   `/studio/leads`.
8. **Domain:** the final domain for `PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.

   service names; please confirm each matches what you actually offer.
