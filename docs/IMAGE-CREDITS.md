## How they were chosen

Subjects: sprinkler pipework, hydrant valves, hose reels and hose boxes, extinguishers, manual call points, sounders and detectors, valve stations, maintenance checks, core drilling. Every candidate was checked by eye at full size and rejected if it showed firefighters, fire engines, burning buildings or flames, a readable maker's name or logo, or signage in a language other than English. Rejected on those grounds during selection: several pull stations and call points carrying maker names, a hose reel and extinguishers with brand labels, and signs in Cyrillic, Chinese, Spanish, German and Malay.
